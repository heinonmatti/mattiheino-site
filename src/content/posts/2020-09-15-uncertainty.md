We tend to treat uncertainty as the enemy of a good decision. More data, more
modelling, more deliberation &ndash; surely that is the path to a better choice.
Often it is the opposite.

When the range of plausible outcomes is wide and irreducible, the rational move
is to stop optimising for a single forecast and start choosing options that
survive across many futures. Robustness beats precision.

This reframing turns a paralysing question (*what exactly will happen?*) into a
tractable one (*which choice do I least regret across the futures I can
imagine?*).
